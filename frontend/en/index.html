His Life in Brief

Bidel was born in 1054 AH in Azimabad, on the banks of the Ganges, into a family of Barlas Turks. His father died before Bidel turned five, and his uncle Mirza Qalandar raised him among dervishes and people of the heart. He wrote poetry from his early youth, and figures such as Shah Kabuli, Shah Maluk and Shah Qasim Huwallahi shaped his outlook.

He spent most of his life between Delhi, Akbarabad (Agra) and Mathura, and finally settled in Delhi. He died there in 1133 AH. His tomb was lost in the invasions of the following century, but his poetry lived on in Afghanistan, Central Asia and the Indian subcontinent.
